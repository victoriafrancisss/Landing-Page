# How to Create Your Automated Welcome Email in ConvertKit

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Go to Automations
1. In your ConvertKit dashboard, click **"Automate"** in the top navigation
2. Click **"Create Automation"** or **"New Automation"**

### Step 2: Set the Trigger
1. Choose **"Form"** as the trigger type
2. Select **"When someone subscribes to a form"**
3. Choose your form: **"Stop the $15,600 Leak: The Automation Audit Checklist"** (or whatever you named it)
4. Click **"Continue"** or **"Next"**

### Step 3: Add the Email Action
1. Click **"Add Action"** or **"Add Step"**
2. Choose **"Send Email"**
3. Click **"Create Email"** or **"New Email"**

### Step 4: Write Your Welcome Email
Fill in these fields:

**Subject Line:**
```
Your Automation Checklist is Here! 🎯
```
(or something similar)

**Email Content:**
You can write something like:
```
Hi there!

Thanks for downloading the Automation Audit Checklist. 

This checklist will help you identify exactly where you're losing time and money to manual processes.

[ATTACH YOUR CHECKLIST HERE - see options below]

In the meantime, I'd love to help you recover those lost hours. Book a free 15-minute audit to see how much time you could save:

[Link to your Calendly: https://calendly.com/victoriafranciss/automation-discovery-call]

Best,
Victoria Francis
Lead Automation Architect
```

### Step 5: Add Your Checklist
You have 3 options:

**Option A: Attach as PDF**
1. In the email editor, look for **"Attachments"** or **"Add Attachment"**
2. Upload your checklist PDF
3. The PDF will be attached to the email automatically

**Option B: Link to Download**
1. Upload your checklist to Google Drive, Dropbox, or your website
2. Get a shareable link
3. In the email, add a button: **"Download Your Checklist"** with the link

**Option C: Include in Email Body**
1. Copy/paste your checklist content directly into the email
2. Format it nicely with headings and bullet points

### Step 6: Save and Activate
1. Click **"Save Email"**
2. Click **"Save Automation"** or **"Activate"**
3. Your automation is now live! ✅

## Testing Your Automation
1. Go to your form in ConvertKit
2. Use a test email address to subscribe
3. Check that email inbox - you should receive your welcome email!

## Pro Tips:
- **Personalization**: Use `{{ subscriber.first_name }}` to personalize (if you collect names)
- **Timing**: The email sends immediately after subscription (you can change this)
- **Follow-ups**: Add more emails to the sequence (e.g., Day 3, Day 7 follow-ups)
- **Links**: Make sure your Calendly link is clickable

## Need to Edit Later?
- Go to **Automate** → Find your automation → Click to edit
- You can update the email content anytime
